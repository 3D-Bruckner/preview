# preview v0.2: a review page: design

Date: 2026-11-27. Status: draft for Marcel's approval. Builds on [the v0.1 design](2026-11-27-preview-design.md), which it changes
in two of its non-goals (feedback, slicing); the decision goes into a new ADR when the branch lands.

## Intent

The customer page becomes a **review page**: the customer looks at what was designed for them, sees what it takes to print
(time, weight, filament per colour), tries colours on the parts and sends back what they want changed. The operator reads that
next to the version it is about. It is not an order: no price, no confirmation, no checkout. Ordering stays where it is today.

Why: Marcel prints multi-plate Bambu Studio projects and designs from the workshop (CT 125). Today's page shows every plate side by
side in grey, says nothing about printing, and every answer comes back by mail or messenger, unstructured. The look should also be
the operator's own: for 3D Bruckner, 3dbruckner.de's.

## Goals

1. **The look** of the [mock](https://claude.ai/artifact/5MwmYhva2nbXAEEA7HSVMK) (Open Design file
   `/root/3D-Bruckner/preview-designs/Open Design/vorschau-app/vorschau-app.html`): white, hairline borders, 6 px radii, one accent,
   bold tight headings, light and dark theme with an Auto/Hell/Dunkel switch, on the customer pages and the web app.
2. **Plates.** "Alle Platten" shows every plate side by side, each numbered; a strip of plate cards (Bambu's own `plate_N.png` when
   the file has it) shows one plate alone and fitted. A file with one plate has no strip.
3. **Parts.** A list per plate (or all): name, filament (material and colour), plate; an eye toggles the part in the 3D view;
   hovering a row highlights the part.
4. **Measurements.** Width × depth × height in mm of the whole model or of the plate shown.
5. **View tools.** 3D / Oben / Vorne / Seite, fit, grid on/off, fullscreen.
6. **Print figures per version:** print time, weight, grams per filament, and per plate when the file has them.
7. **Colour wishes and a comment.** Each part gets a picker of the operator's filaments; the 3D view recolours live; "Rückmeldung
   senden" stores the wish with an optional comment against that version. The operator gets a mail and sees it in the web app;
   Claude reads it over MCP; the customer sees what they sent.

## Non-goals

- A price on the customer page, ordering, accepting a quote, payment.
- Slicing again when a colour changes; slicing per plate through the service.
- Customers changing geometry or parameters; a download of any file.
- Replies or threads: one feedback is one message to the operator; the conversation continues through the usual channel.
- Export to Bambuddy or Manyfold (still follow-ups).

## Anti-goals

- The customer link must not become a way to store arbitrary data or send mail at will (rate limits, size limits, validation against
  the version's own parts and the operator's filaments).
- The product names no operator: the slicer, the filaments and the look's brand parts are settings or data, never code.
- A customer must not be offered a colour the operator can't print.

## Constraints

- v0.1's rules hold (`CLAUDE.md`, `docs/architecture.md`): one Next.js app, framework-free `backend/`, every read of the customer
  pages starts from `customerByToken`, MCP and REST mirror each other, de/en copy, appended migrations only, no tokens, link tokens,
  file names or email addresses in logs.
- Conventions: HTTPS clients (timeouts, no retries storms), one field list per field shared by MCP/REST/UI, accessibility (focus,
  contrast, motion), icons as outline line icons.
- The slicing service (`3D-Bruckner/slicing`, live at `slicing.3dbruckner.de`): `POST /estimate` with `file` (3MF/STL ≤ 50 MB) and
  `material` (PLA/PETG); answers grams, minutes, `filaments[{slot,type,color,grams}]` and a price; sums every plate; one slice at a
  time with 5 waiting (503 `busy`), 10 per hour per caller unless the token lifts it, 120 s timeout; fixed A1 profiles.

## Approaches (decided with Marcel)

**Print figures: mixed (chosen)** — read them from the file when it is a sliced Bambu project (`Metadata/slice_info.config`:
per plate, from the operator's own profiles), and call the slicing service only for files without them (workshop exports, STL).
*Always the service* was simpler but gives whole-model figures only, on fixed profiles, even when the file knows better.

**Feedback: in preview (chosen)** — stored against the version, mailed to the operator, shown in the web app and over MCP.
*A pre-filled mail* stored nothing and needed no protection, but the wishes would live in the inbox, not next to the model, and
the customer could edit them freely.

**Price shown: no** (Marcel: "first only a review page").

## Design

### What preview reads from a 3MF (at upload, on the server)

A new `backend/threemf/` reads the uploaded 3MF once, in `addVersion`, and stores the result as the version's **facts** (JSON):

- **Plates:** from `Metadata/model_settings.config` (`plate` → `plater_id`, its objects). No such file (an OpenSCAD or plain 3MF):
  one plate holding every build item.
- **Parts:** per plate, each object, and within an object each part that has its own filament (`extruder` metadata), with its
  name; the key is stable within the version (`plate:object:part`).
- **Filament slots:** `Metadata/project_settings.config` (`filament_colour`, `filament_type`); a plain 3MF: its `basematerials` /
  `colorgroup` colours if any, else one slot with no colour.
- **Plate pictures:** `Metadata/plate_N.png` stored as version files, served like previews.
- **Figures, when sliced:** `Metadata/slice_info.config` per plate (`prediction` seconds, `weight` grams, filament `used_g` per
  slot); summed for the whole model.

The upload's `preview` field becomes optional when the file has `plate_1.png` (it is used as the preview).

Measurements are computed in the browser from the loaded geometry (bounding box of the visible parts, in mm); the server does not
parse meshes.

### Slicing for files without figures

- Settings: `PREVIEW_SLICER_URL` and `PREVIEW_SLICER_TOKEN` (optional; unset: no figures for such files, nothing else changes),
  `PREVIEW_SLICER_MATERIAL` default when the file names none (PLA).
- After `addVersion` stores a version without figures, it records a **slice job** (`pending`). One in-process worker takes pending
  jobs one at a time (also those left from before a restart, at start), posts the 3MF, and stores the answer as the facts' figures
  (`source: "slicer"`, whole model only) or the job as `failed` with the reason. A 503/429 is tried again later with backoff,
  at most 5 times over an hour; a 422 is final.
- The customer page shows "wird berechnet" while pending and nothing when failed; the web app shows the failure and a "Neu
  berechnen" button (also an MCP tool).

### The operator's filaments

- A table of **filaments** (name, material, colour `#rrggbb`, available yes/no, position), edited in the web app's settings and
  over MCP/REST (`list_filaments`, `create_filament`, `update_filament`, `delete_filament`).
- The picker offers the available filaments, plus "wie im Entwurf" (the file's own colour). No filaments defined: no pickers and
  no colour part of the feedback, the comment still works.
- A model's figures show the file's colours and material; the filament names come from the list when a colour and material match
  an entry exactly, else "PLA · #00AE42".

### Feedback

- Stored per version: customer, time, comment (≤ 2000 characters, optional), and colour wishes `[{part, filament}]` validated
  against the version's facts and the available filaments. At least one of the two.
- Sent from the model page through a server action that starts from `customerByToken` and refuses a version that isn't that
  customer's (one gate, like every read).
- Limits: 10 per customer per hour and 50 per day (`429` with a German message); body size bounded by the comment limit and the
  number of parts.
- A mail to `PREVIEW_FEEDBACK_EMAIL` (optional; unset: no mail) through the configured SMTP: model, version, the wishes as a
  list, the comment, a link into the web app. No customer email is collected.
- For 3D Bruckner (deployment, not product), Marcel 2026-11-27: `PREVIEW_FEEDBACK_EMAIL=feedback@3dbruckner.de`, delivered to
  the one mailbox `webmaster@3dbruckner.de` (Strato) by its catch-all, no alias needed; sent as the existing `noreply@3dbruckner.de` (`PREVIEW_SMTP_URL` with that
  login, `PREVIEW_SMTP_FROM=3D Bruckner <noreply@3dbruckner.de>`; 1Password Business "Strato noreply@3dbruckner.de"). Invoice
  Ninja sends from the same mailbox, so a new password there is a change to both. Production has no SMTP setting for preview yet;
  SPF, DKIM and DMARC are checked with one real mail.
- Web app: on the model page, each version's feedback (newest first), marked seen when opened; a count of unseen on the start
  page. MCP/REST: `list_feedback` (filter by customer, model, unseen), `mark_feedback_seen`.
- The customer page shows their own sent feedback under the version ("Deine Rückmeldung vom 27.11.2026"), read-only.

### The customer pages

As in the mock:

- **Overview (`/k/<token>`):** "Deine Modelle", one card per model with its preview, plates and parts count, latest version,
  date and note; "Neue Version" when the latest is newer than 7 days.
- **Model (`/k/<token>/<model>`):** breadcrumb, title and description, version picker; the viewer (toolbar, plate strip,
  measurements chip, axis gizmo, controls hint) next to a side panel: Maße, Druck (time, weight, per filament), Teile (with pickers
  and eyes), Versionen, Zeichnungen, Rückmeldung (comment + send; shows the colour wishes picked above).
- Phone: everything stacks, the plate strip scrolls sideways, the side panel follows the viewer.
- The viewer: three.js as today, the 3MF's parts as separate meshes keyed like the facts, materials coloured from the filament slots
  (`filament_colour` by each part's extruder); a picked colour recolours the mesh.

### The look

- Hanken Grotesk (OFL), self-hosted, becomes the product's font; the accent stays `PREVIEW_ACCENT_COLOR`; the logo stays
  `PREVIEW_LOGO` (3D Bruckner's ruler mark is part of its SVG). Dark theme derived as today, with the switch Auto/Hell/Dunkel
  (stored in a cookie like the language).
- The web app gets the same header, cards and type; its screens otherwise stay as they are.

## Claude's calls (check while reading)

- Part granularity is object, split by filament when an object has parts on different extruders.
- Measurements in the browser, not on the server.
- Slicer retries: 5 over an hour for 503/429; 422 is final.
- Feedback limits 10/hour and 50/day per customer, comment ≤ 2000 characters.
- `PREVIEW_FEEDBACK_EMAIL` as its own setting rather than mailing every allowed address.
- "Neue Version" for 7 days.
- Facts are computed once at upload and stored; a parser fix later needs a re-read (a `reread_facts` MCP tool), not a migration.

## Left to the builder

- The exact XML/JSON paths in the 3MF parts, proven against a made-up 2-plate fixture (Bambu-style: `model_settings.config`,
  `project_settings.config`, `slice_info.config`, `plate_1.png`, `plate_2.png`) and today's `ramp.3mf` / `keychain.3mf`.
- Mapping three.js `ThreeMFLoader`'s output to part keys (or a small own loader for Bambu's `3D/Objects/*.model` components).
- The worker's place in the process (`instrumentation.ts`) and its tests with a fake slicer.
- Splitting this into plans: likely (1) look, (2) facts + plates + parts + measurements, (3) figures + slicer, (4) filaments +
  feedback.
