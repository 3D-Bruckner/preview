# preview v0.3: desktop layouts and polish: design

Date: 2026-11-28. Status: approved by Marcel through four rounds on the mock (below); built on branch `v0.3`. Builds on
[the v0.2 design](2026-11-27-preview-v0.2-design.md).

## Intent

v0.2 is one narrow phone column everywhere. Marcel works at a desktop: he adds customers, adds models to send to people and reads their
feedback per model. The customer's page hides the Rückmeldung card at the bottom right, shows the version twice and gives no sign that a
part is focused. v0.3 gives each screen a desktop layout that still stacks to one column on a phone, and records when a customer looked.

## The mock

`customer-model.html`, `preview.css`. Private previews: [Kunden](https://claude.ai/artifact/3ie2KfgTFoLG734GB9YraE),

## Goals

1. **Kunden (web app start).** Two columns on a desktop: the customers (search, "Neuer Kunde", a dot per customer with unseen
   feedback) on the left; the picked customer on the right. Phone: the list, then the customer on its own page.
2. **The customer card.** Name and note with a pencil (edit in a dialog: name, note, language). The link box: the link, **Link
   kopieren**, **Öffnen** (new tab), **Neuer Link** (asks first); **Link sperren** stays, smaller. Under it **An <Name> schicken:
   WhatsApp · Mail**: `https://wa.me/?text=` and `mailto:?subject=&body=` with a prepared text in the customer's language and the
   link; Marcel presses send himself (preview sends nothing). A line says when the customer last opened their link.
3. **The customer's models.** One row each: title, newest version and its day, parts, "angesehen <wann>" or "noch nicht angesehen",
   feedback count (new ones marked); each row opens the model. **Neues Modell:** title, description and the first 3MF (PNG optional)
   in one form.
4. **The model page (admin).** Breadcrumb, title with a pencil (title and description in a dialog), **Wie <Name> es sieht** (the
   customer's model page, new tab) and **Link kopieren**. Versions as cards: preview picture, note, print time and weight, when the
   customer viewed that version ("Anna: heute, 09:41"), and that version's feedback inline (new ones tinted, colour wishes as
   chips). Beside them: **Neue Version** (3MF, PNG optional, drawings, note). No separate Modell box.
5. **When the customer looked.** preview records the last time a customer opened their link and, per version, the last time they
   viewed it. Opens by someone signed in to the web app (Marcel's "Öffnen", "Wie Anna es sieht") don't count. Shown as in 2–4,
   and in MCP and REST (`last_seen_at` on a customer, `seen_at` on a version).
6. **The PNG preview is optional.** Without one, the upload takes the picture Bambu Studio put in the 3MF (`Metadata/plate_1.png`,
   else `Metadata/thumbnail.png`); a 3MF with neither still needs one.
7. **The customer's model page.** The 3D view large, with **Maße** and **Druck** side by side under it; the side panel is
   **Teile & Farben** (scrolls after about six rows) over **Deine Rückmeldung**, which stays in sight. One version picker by the
   title, "Neu in Version n: <note>" under it; the Versionen card goes. Drawings stay, under Maße and Druck.
8. **Several parts at once.** The focus button adds a part to what is shown; × takes it out. A bar over the view names what is shown
   ("Nur Box + Deckel") with **Ganzes Modell ×**; the shown rows are tinted with an accent bar, the others dimmed. Maße measure what
   is shown. The eye (hide a part) goes: showing some parts covers it.
9. **Language switch as flags**, each named by its language in its own words (`lang`, `aria-label`).

## Non-goals

- The multi-plate slicing gap, all-black parts without shading and the tile previews (handoff "Found"): their own change.
- A per-customer history of opens: only the last one is kept.
- Sending the link from preview: Marcel sends it with his own mail and WhatsApp.

## Constraints

v0.1/v0.2's rules hold (`CLAUDE.md`, `docs/architecture.md`): every customer read starts from `customerByToken`; MCP and REST mirror
each other and share one field list (`backend/shapes.ts`); de/en copy, English first; appended migrations only; no tokens, file names
or email addresses in logs. Conventions: lucide icons through `components/icons.tsx`, WCAG 2.2 AA (never colour alone, 24 px targets,
390 px phones, light and dark).

## Design

### Views (backend)

A new migration `views` adds `customers.seen_at` and a table `version_views (version_id primary key → versions, seen_at)`. The
customer pages call `recordView(rt, token, versionId?)` (backend/views.ts), which starts from `customerByToken` and checks the version
is the customer's, and is skipped when the request has an operator session (`rt.person(headers)`). A model's "angesehen" is the
newest `seen_at` of its versions. Links that lead to a model page from the customer's pages do not prefetch (a prefetch would count
as a view, as one already would mark feedback seen on the admin side).

### Preview picture from the 3MF

`addVersion`'s `preview` becomes optional. Without it, `backend/threemf` reads `Metadata/plate_1.png` or `Metadata/thumbnail.png`
(bounded like every entry, ≤ 16 MiB, checked to be a PNG) and stores it as the version's `preview.png`.

### Admin files

The admin version cards show the preview through a new signed-in route `app/api/versions/[id]/preview` (outside the proxy; 401 without a
session or token).

### Layout

The web app's main column widens to 1240 px; the menu is a row under the header from 768 px and stays the bottom tab bar on a phone.
The customer's model page is `1fr | 360px` from 1024 px with a sticky side panel; below it stacks: view, Maße and Druck, parts,
feedback, drawings.
